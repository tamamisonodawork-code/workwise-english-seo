FAQ

Questions before you start

Is Workwise English an AI correction tool?

No. Workwise English is a workplace English practice app focused on culture, tone, soft feedback, and what to say next.

Who is it for?

It is for intermediate English learners, immigrants, and international professionals who want to feel more confident in North American workplaces.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is free for learners and does not require email login, credit card, or app installation.

How is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved in this browser. Use Export / Import if you want to back up your learning data or move it to another device.

Does Workwise English record or upload my voice?

No. Workwise English includes text-to-speech for listening and shadowing, but it does not require voice upload.
