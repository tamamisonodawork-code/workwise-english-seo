FAQ

Questions before you start

Is Workwise English an AI correction tool?

No. It is a workplace English practice app focused on culture, tone, soft feedback, and what to say next.

Who is it for?

Intermediate English learners, immigrants, and international professionals who want to feel more confident in North American workplaces.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is free for learners and does not require email login, credit card, or app installation.

Does Workwise English record or upload my voice?

No. It includes text-to-speech for listening and shadowing, but it does not require voice upload.
